# Investor Narrative

**Company:** isteBul · **Stage:** Seed · **Market:** Turkey · **Config:** `data/investor/investor-narrative.json`

---

## One line

**Decision infrastructure for high-consideration purchases** — starting with automotive in Turkey, expanding across housing, finance, insurance, and travel on a shared engine.

---

## Problem

Consumers make **₺500K–₺3M** decisions with fragmented tools: classifieds show inventory, banks show a single product, and generic AI invents prices. Nobody unifies **fit, total cost of ownership, finance load, and neutral execution** in one auditable workflow.

**Buyer truth:** *"I need to know if this car fits my budget and life — not just the monthly payment the dealer quoted."*

---

## Solution

| Pillar | What we ship |
|--------|----------------|
| **Decide** | Multi-factor match, confidence tiers, TCO profiles |
| **Explain** | LLM narrates deterministic numbers — cannot override scores |
| **Close** | Qualified leads → HMAC webhooks → CRM → outcome feedback |

**Demo:** https://www.istebul.com/auto — scores update as inputs change.

---
